## What is SHIKHARA?

**SHIKHARA** (शिखर) is a premium Indian traditional fashion brand founded by **Shikhar**. The name means *"the spire of a temple — the highest, most sacred point."* It is the meeting of ancient Indian craft and modern luxury — a brand that stands at the summit.

SHIKHARA is positioned at the level of global luxury houses — Gucci, Balenciaga, Versace — but rooted entirely in the soul of India.

## Heritage — Crafts by State

### Kerala
- Kasavu sarees (gold border)
- Traditional Mundu & Neriyathu
- Kerala mural-inspired prints

### Gujarat
- Bandhani (tie-dye)
- Mirror-work (Shisha embroidery)
- Patola silk weaving
- Kutchi embroidery

### West Bengal
- Banarasi & Baluchari silks
- Kantha embroidery
- Muslin (Jamdani)
- Tant cotton

### Madhya Pradesh
- Chanderi silk-cotton
- Maheshwari weaves
- Bagh block printing
- Batik art

### Rajasthan
- Leheriya (wave dyeing)
- Block printing (Bagru, Sanganer)
- Zardozi gold embroidery
- Bandhej

### Punjab
- Phulkari (flower embroidery)
- Dupatta art
- Patiala suits

### Karnataka
- Mysore silk
- Ilkal sarees
- Lambani embroidery

### Tamil Nadu
- Kanchipuram (Kanjivaram) silk
- Chettinad cotton
- Kalamkari prints

## Brand Trademark Note

**SHIKHARA** as a fashion brand name is currently unregistered as a major trademark in the fashion space (Class 25 — Clothing & Accessories). To protect your brand:

1. Visit **ipindia.gov.in** → Trademark → Public Search → Search "Shikhara" → Class 25
2. If clear, file a trademark application under Class 25 (clothing) and Class 14 (jewellery/watches)
3. You may use the **™** symbol immediately without registration
4. You may only use **®** after official registration is granted
