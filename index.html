Our Story

Born at the Shikhar of India

🪔
✦
SHIKHARA
"Every thread tells a story. Every stitch carries a civilization."

SHIKHARA is not merely a fashion brand. It is an act of reverence — to the weavers of Varanasi, the dyers of Rajasthan, the embroiderers of Gujarat, the silk masters of Tamil Nadu. Founded by Shikhar with the belief that Indian craft deserves the same altar as any European house.

We source from the hands that have kept traditions alive for centuries, and we present them in the language of modern luxury. The result is something the world has not seen before — Indian heritage, uncompromised.

Heritage Crafts

Woven from Across India

01
🌴

Kerala

Kasavu Weaving

The iconic gold-bordered white fabric — worn at celebrations, woven by hand on traditional looms along the backwaters.

02
🪞

Gujarat

Bandhani & Mirror Work

Tie-dye mastery and Shisha embroidery from the Kutch craftspeople — each mirror reflecting a universe of colour.

03
🧵

West Bengal

Kantha & Muslin

Running stitch artistry and the legendary Jamdani muslin — so fine it was once called "woven air" by Mughal emperors.

04
🖼️

Madhya Pradesh

Chanderi & Bagh Print

Centuries-old silk-cotton weaves from Chanderi and the natural dye block printing tradition of Bagh village.

05
👑

Rajasthan

Zardozi & Leheriya

Gold thread embroidery fit for royalty, paired with the wave-pattern tie-dye of the desert — Leheriya.

06
🌸

Punjab

Phulkari

Flower embroidery done entirely in silk floss on coarse fabric — a tradition passed from mother to daughter for generations.

07
🏛️

Karnataka

Mysore Silk

Pure mulberry silk from the royal city — lustrous, lightweight, and carrying the weight of centuries of Carnatic culture.

08
🎨

Tamil Nadu

Kanjivaram Silk

The queen of silks — woven with gold and silver zari, bright colours, and contrasting borders since the Chola dynasty.
