Frequently Asked

Do you do custom / bespoke orders?

Yes. SHIKHARA accepts custom orders for all categories — Men's, Women's, Jewellery, and Watches. Contact us via phone or email with your requirements and we'll work with you personally.

What states / crafts do you source from?

We source from Kerala, Gujarat, West Bengal, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Punjab, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Odisha. Each piece is tied to a specific regional craft tradition.

How do I place an order?

Currently, orders are placed via direct contact — call +91 91062 31032 or email devildeniedd2@gmail.com. A full e-commerce system is coming soon.

Is SHIKHARA only for Indian customers?

Not at all. SHIKHARA ships and serves customers across India and globally. Indian heritage fashion has no borders.
